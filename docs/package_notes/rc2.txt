ffxiii2-nx package rc2 (2026-11-05): the fixes from your rc1 console run.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE rc1
1. UI on the Switch: the garbled dynamic text, battle HUD gauges and save-screen thumbnails came from one of banjo-nx's
   deko3d enhancements, the pushed uniform buffers (banjo_deko3d_push_constants). Bisected in Ryujinx (20 keys -> group
   of 5 -> one key); it is off in this build (built in). Ryujinx: title, "Begin game" dialog and save screen render as
   on the PC. Still wrong: the Primer's picture box (a separate bug, under investigation).
2. CPU: the effect job system's four worker threads spun in polling loops (~240 % of the three cores, the main thread
   got 14 %). The game's pure polling loops now give the core up while they wait (CPU yield hint, then core yields,
   then 25 us sleeps; the loops read and leave exactly as before). Key ffxiii2_spin_backoff (on); A/B target 19.
3. Shaders: the 37 shaders your console captured are in the package; deko3d_shaders.bin is compiled on the PC from your
   console's pipeline list and the PC's (1,919 variants, merged with your console's own cache), so far fewer draws wait
   for a shader compile (rc1 stalled for seconds at new scenes).
4. 209 hot-function copies (verified on the PC: 0 differences), as rc1.
