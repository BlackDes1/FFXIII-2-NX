ffxiii2-nx package rc1 (2026-11-05): the first console package of Final Fantasy XIII-2.

WHAT IS IN THIS BUILD
- Renut's renderer on deko3d (no NVK linked), the same picture as the PC build (pixel-identical defaults).
- 209 hot-function copies (machine track, localized_bulk.txt): batch 1 (177, from Ryujinx r8 and PC prof1) and
  batch 2 (32, from Ryujinx r10). Both verified on the PC with the guard on every call: verify2 177/177 reached,
  118.4 M calls, 0 differences; verify3 209/209 reached, 127.6 M calls, 0 differences.
- No hand-written natives yet, no PGO/LTO (pgo-lto.md: they come after your console profile).
- NRO 65.7 MB, ELF 83.0 MB; heap at the renderer's start in Ryujinx: 3,202 MB free.

TESTED IN RYUJINX (r11, the picture only; the console decides performance)
- Boot, title, main menu, the Primer, the opening cutscene in 3D. 60 fps in the menus, 30 fps (the game's own cap)
  in the cutscene, in the emulator.

KNOWN ISSUES ON DEKO3D (Ryujinx)
- The copyright line under the title is smeared; one credit reads "DIRECTOR / DIRECTOR"; a cyan panel appeared over
  the title logo once. The Primer's picture box showed a whole sheet of button icons (to be compared with the PC).
- On the PC build an intermittent stall at "New save data is required" was seen once.
